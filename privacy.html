Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

WaitReally Studio is an internal publishing tool used only by the owner of the Wait, Really? (@yesreallytv) channel to upload its own videos to its own social media accounts and to read those accounts' statistics and comments.

What data the tool accesses

When the channel owner connects an account, the tool receives an access token from the platform (for example TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Threads or Facebook) and uses it to upload videos, read basic profile information, read post statistics, and read or reply to comments on the channel's own posts.

How data is stored

Access tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's private computer. They are not sold, shared or transferred to any third party, and they are not used for advertising.

Other people's data

The tool does not collect personal data from viewers. Public comments on the channel's own posts may be read so the owner can reply to them; they are not stored beyond what is needed to reply.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time from each platform's app settings, which makes the stored tokens useless. Stored data is deleted when access is revoked.
